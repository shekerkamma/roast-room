# Brief: How to make money from the content-ideas plugin

**The decision:** how to turn the content-ideas plugin into revenue in the next 90 days.

**Options**
- **A. Keep it free and open; use it as a lead magnet** for the founder's AI consulting and pre-sales work.
- **B. Paid hosted version:** a $29/month self-serve plan (the daily For You feed, run for the user, no setup).
- **C. Done-for-you setup:** a $2,000 one-time install plus 30 days of tuning for consultants and small agencies.
- **D. Keep doing what I do now:** no monetization, keep building skills.

**The business:** the content-ideas plugin runs in Claude Code and Codex. It scrapes competitors on X, Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube and turns what performs into a daily feed of content ideas. It sits in a repo of about 375 skills the founder also uses for AI strategy, pre-sales decks, and consulting delivery. Current plugin revenue: $0 (assumed). Consulting revenue: about $15k a month (assumed). Team: the founder alone, with AI agents (assumed).

**The resources:** about 10 founder hours a week for this (assumed); $5k cash set aside (assumed); a 90-day window.

**What success looks like:** $5k a month in new revenue that the plugin can be credited with, inside 90 days, without cutting consulting hours.

**The lean:** the founder leans toward **B**, because recurring revenue compounds and a hosted plan scales without their time. They worry that the scraping cost (a paid ScrapeCreators API key per user) and support load could eat the margin.
