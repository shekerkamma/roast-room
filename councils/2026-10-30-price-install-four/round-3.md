## Chair

Vote: 0 endorse, 6 endorse with amendment, 0 dissent.

**Amendments**
1. Cap the guarantee (Buffett, Welsh). **Adopted.** Both seats showed that "tuning continues free" cancels the 5-hour tuning cap, and no seat argued otherwise. The seats differ on the number: Welsh's 3 more free hours is used because it protects the 10-hour week, and Buffett's $1,000 partial refund is used because Welsh named no refund amount.
2. Cap installs at 2 a month at any price (Martell). **Adopted.** Nobody objected, and the calendar math (3 installs at 13 to 15 hours fill a 43-hour month) went unanswered.
3. Track a weekly usable-ideas count in the time log, and get install 1's day-14 count (Martell, Welsh). **Adopted** as a measurement that backs the guarantee and the sales copy. It is not a gate on the price.
4. Make the hours log an input to the price, not a gate (Graham). **Adopted.** At 13 hours, capacity alone justifies the raise (Graham, backed by Martell's calendar math), and Buffett's Round 3 card did not defend the 13-hour gate. The log still sets the hourly case and the tuning cap.
5. Define the 30% close rate as closes divided by calls held, not messages sent (Graham). **Adopted.** 3 closes from 20 messages is 15%, which would fail a gate the brief calls success. No objection.
6. Raise the December test from 5 prospects to 10 (Hormozi). **Adopted.** His odds argument (zero closes from 5 quotes 17% of the time at a 30% close rate) went unanswered.
7. Tell November prospects now that the price goes to $3,500 on 2026-12-01 (Hormozi). **Not adopted.** It commits to the raise before November's gate clears, which undoes the control that Hormozi, Martell and Welsh accepted from Graham in Round 2. Recorded in the minority report.
8. Build the runbook whatever November shows; pass C at 1.5 hours or less of founder time per tester, rewrite it between 1.5 and 3, kill it above 3 (Naval). **Adopted.** No objection.
