## Hormozi

SEAT: HORMOZI (Offers, pricing, and sales)
STANCE: Strong yes
OPTION SCORES: A 3/10 · B 9/10 · C 5/10 · D 1/10
PILLAR SCORE: 7/10
CONFIDENCE: 4/5
RUIN RISK: none
DOOR: two-way

THE CALL: B, but bigger: message all 20 warm contacts this week with a done-for-you offer and a service guarantee, and ask every one of them for two names whether they buy or not.

WHY:
1. The constraint is leads, and warm outreach is the cheapest of the Core Four. D isn't a channel at all: "if you're not spending your day doing one of those core four things, you are not advertising." (Core Four, S070). Polishing with zero sales is hiding.
2. Let's do the math. I'm assuming 20 contacts, 50% reply, 10 calls, and a 30% close, which gets you 3 sales. That's a list with no slack. Two names from each person adds 40 warm-ish leads, and the same rates on those give you about 6 more. "Referrals are both worth worth more and cost less, which is a dangerous combo for making money as a business." (Lead getters, S112). Cash CAC is about $0, so even small numbers clear 3 to 1 easily.
3. C sounds like leverage, and partners are nodes (Lead getters, S112). But an agency owner won't push an offer with zero installs and zero proof. Get 3 case studies first and C scores 8 next month. A at under 2,000 followers can't reach the volume to judge in 30 days (Rule of 100, S002).

THE RISK I SEE: $2,000 is underpriced. Say install plus 30 days of tuning takes 15 hours. At your $15k-a-month consulting rate that's real cost, and you're well under 5 to 10x delivery (S094). If three installs eat your consulting hours, you've traded a good business for a worse one.

WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY MIND: Fewer than 5 of the 20 replying in 7 days. Then the offer is the problem, not the channel, and I'd rebuild it before touching anything else (Grand Slam Offer, S043).

WHAT I'D DO THIS WEEK: Send 20 personal messages offering the install with a guarantee: if the feed doesn't give them usable ideas in 30 days, you keep tuning until it does (Guarantees, S026). Book calls and run them with questions, not demos (CLOSER, S069).

QUESTION FOR THE FOUNDER: How many hours does one install plus 30 days of tuning actually take you?

## Graham

STANCE: Strong yes

PILLAR SCORE: 7/10
CONFIDENCE: 4/5
RUIN RISK: none
DOOR: two-way

THE CALL: B, but run it as a demand test. Talk to all 20 consultants one by one this week, and show each of them a feed built on their own competitors before you ask for $2,000.

WHY:
1. Nobody has bought this yet, so the only question that matters is whether anyone wants it. Twenty conversations answer that in two weeks. LinkedIn posts don't, and an agency partner doesn't either. Recruiting users yourself is the core of (Do things that don't scale, S096), and the scoring rule there is blunt: low if you are "waiting on a launch, a partnership, or a hire to bring users." A and C are both forms of waiting.
2. The arithmetic. 3 sales out of 20 needs a 15% close rate. That's high for strangers and reasonable for warm contacts, if the pain is real. Assume 2 hours per contact for outreach, a call and a custom demo feed. That's 40 hours, which is exactly 4 weeks at 10 hours a week. A with under 2,000 followers (assumed) can't get there in 30 days. C gives away $600 a sale to partners who have never sold this and have no reason to push it yet. "Partnerships too usually don't work." (S096)
3. D is the classic mistake. "Launching too slowly has probably killed a hundred times more startups than launching too fast" (S178). The first three buyers will show you what to polish.
4. A concession: this is a services offer, not a startup, so the growth reflex fits it less well. But with $15k a month coming in, the founder is default alive either way and the $5k never gets touched (S080). So the only real risk here is wasted time.

THE RISK I SEE: Friends say yes to be kind. Three polite payments from your own network prove loyalty, not demand, and then the 4th sale never happens.

WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY MIND: Fewer than 5 of the 20 agreeing to a call in week one, or nobody asking an unprompted follow-up question after seeing their own feed. Either would mean the pain isn't sharp enough, and I'd stop selling installs and go find out why.

WHAT I'D DO THIS WEEK: Build a sample feed for the 5 warmest contacts, using their real competitors, and send each one personally with the price stated.

Add 10 hours of selling and you're at 40 of the 43. "I use this thing called the camcorder method I literally record myself doing the work while I'm doing the work so it takes me no extra time."
