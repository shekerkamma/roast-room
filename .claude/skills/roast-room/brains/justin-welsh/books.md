# Books: Justin Welsh

Built from the extract notes for S001 to S083 (his weekly essays, January 2022 to October 2026). No book by him appears in the corpus; his teaching lives in the weekly essays and his paid courses. Summaries are in my own words, and no product or book text is reproduced.

## His own work: essay series and courses

### The weekly Saturday essays (2022 to 2026)
- **Core thesis:** A one-person business can be built on content and simple products, and should be shaped around the life you want, not around maximum growth.
- **Key frameworks:** two eras. Tactical era (2022 to 2024): LinkedIn openers, thread systems, templates and a content library, funnels, launch segmentation, pricing ladders, the 5 phases of solopreneurship. Reflective era (2025 to 2026): enough, written non-negotiables, the what-if-it-works test, the easy version first, curiosity as signal, freedom as chosen constraints. He says the shift away from tactical topics cost revenue (S035).
- **Taught in sources:** all of S001 to S083; first issue S083 (2022-01-08).

### The LinkedIn Playbook (2020)
- **Core thesis:** Teach people to grow on LinkedIn; his first product, made rough on purpose.
- **Key frameworks:** ugliest possible version first (about 90 hours, webcam and slides, $50, one launch post); built in time freed by raising consulting rates. Made $300 on day one and about $75,000 over 18 months, and was later retired.
- **Taught in sources:** S006, S040.

### The LinkedIn Operating System (LinkedIn OS)
- **Core thesis:** The relaunched, fuller version of the LinkedIn course.
- **Key frameworks:** daily content on one platform, reusable templates, the low-price trust tripwire with an ARPU around $130 (S082). Made over $4.5M (S040). He discontinued it and the Content OS, about 75% of typical monthly revenue, during his 2025 to 2026 change of direction (S033).
- **Taught in sources:** S033, S040, S082, S083.

### The Content Operating System (Content OS, 2022)
- **Core thesis:** A system for producing content without daily inspiration.
- **Key frameworks:** the 730-day library and grading winners (S066). Its launch is his own failure case: expected $200K to $250K, did about $55K because he winged the marketing (S043).
- **Taught in sources:** S033, S043, S066.

### The Creator MBA (launched around the turn of 2024)
- **Core thesis:** A full flagship course for solo creators, at a higher price than his earlier products.
- **Key frameworks:** the segmented, personalized launch (survey, personas, 5% to 10% of each email swapped by segment). About $1.6M in sales, 2,850 purchases, $566 average transaction (S058). S043 dates the first launch email to October 2023; S058 says the launch was in January 2024.
- **Taught in sources:** S043, S058.

### The Monthly Templates
- **Core thesis:** A subscription product, his example of the subscription-email side hustle.
- **Key frameworks:** recurring delivery that scales without more work but churns if any issue disappoints. 2,250+ customers at the time.
- **Taught in sources:** S079.

## Books and pieces he cites (not his)

- **Don't Sweat the Small Stuff** (Richard Carlson): he says the book is more careful than its popular version, and argues for recovering faster rather than not caring. S026.
- **Fire Bullets, Then Cannonballs** (Jim Collins, an article): test small, then commit big; he adopts it for leaving a working line. S027.
- **David Brooks's second-mountain idea** (book not named in the source): a later life built around impact and challenge. S033.
- **The Way of Excellence** (Brad Stulberg): progress motivates early, and later only love of the craft and the people keeps you going. S035.
- **Russell Brunson's books** (no title given): what made him think his LinkedIn DMs could become a digital product. S040.
- **The 5 Types of Wealth** (Sahil Bloom): doing the basics very well beats shortcuts. S051.
- **The PASTOR copy method** (Ray Edwards): his main promotional writing formula since 2019. S060.
